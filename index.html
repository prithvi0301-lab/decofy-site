Why uPVC

Plywood worries?
uPVC doesn't have any.

Unplasticised PVC is the modern alternative to wood and plywood — engineered for kitchens that see water every day, wardrobes that last decades, and walls that stay beautiful without upkeep.

100% Waterproof

Water, steam, spills — nothing soaks in. uPVC never swells, warps or peels, making it perfect for kitchens and humid climates.

Termite & Borer Proof

Pests simply can't eat uPVC. No chemical treatments, no anxiety, no hollowed-out panels after a few monsoons.

Zero Maintenance

No polishing, no repainting, no varnish every other year. A damp cloth keeps it looking brand new.

Fire Retardant

uPVC is self-extinguishing by nature — it resists ignition and doesn't fuel a flame the way plywood does.

Eco-Friendly

Lead-free, 100% recyclable and no trees cut. A cleaner choice for your home and the planet.

20-Year Warranty

Colour-fast and corrosion-free — it won't fade, rust or rot. Every decofy interior is backed by a 20-year warranty.
