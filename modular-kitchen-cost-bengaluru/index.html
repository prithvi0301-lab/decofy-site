What affects modular kitchen cost in Bengaluru?

A useful estimate comes from the actual design and scope. Use this guide to understand the decisions that move the budget before requesting a project-specific quote.

The six main cost drivers

1. Layout and cabinet volume

A straight kitchen typically needs less cabinetry than a larger L-shaped, U-shaped or parallel layout. Wall length alone is not enough; overhead, base and tall-unit volume all matter.

2. Internal storage

Drawers, corner mechanisms, pull-outs, bottle units and tall pantry systems add different levels of utility and cost. Prioritise accessories by daily use.

3. Shutters and finishes

The selected finish, profiles and visual detailing affect the final specification. Compare samples in the room's actual light where possible.

4. Hardware and appliances

Hinges, channels, handles and appliance integration should be included consistently when comparing quotes.

5. Countertop, sink and services

Check whether the scope includes countertop, sink, plumbing, electrical changes, civil work or removal of an existing kitchen.

6. Site access and readiness

Lift access, working hours, unfinished services and pending civil work can affect sequencing. A measured site review makes the estimate more reliable.

How to compare kitchen quotations

  • Compare the same cabinet and accessory scope.
  • Confirm material specification for carcasses and shutters.
  • Separate included items from allowances and exclusions.
  • Ask what the warranty covers; Decofy's 20-year uPVC panel warranty covers discolouration and degradation.
  • Confirm the installation schedule; Decofy's average is 15 days, subject to final scope and site readiness.
