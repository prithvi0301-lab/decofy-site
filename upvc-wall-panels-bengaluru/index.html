Quick answer

uPVC wall panels are wall coverings made from uPVC. Decofy offers them in Bengaluru for selected interior spaces including TV-unit feature walls, living areas and rooms. Decofy's uPVC material is 100% waterproof. Decofy's uPVC material is termite-proof. Decofy provides a 20-year uPVC panel warranty against discolouration and degradation.

Applications

Decofy wall panels can be used:

  • on TV units
  • in rooms
  • in living areas
  • in other selected interior spaces

Finishes

The Decofy panel catalogue includes these finish families:

  • Solid
  • Optima

The warranty

Part of your interiors

Wall panels can be planned with other Decofy uPVC interiors, or as the wall finish alongside a kitchen or wardrobe project.

Before you enquire

  • Note the wall or unit you have in mind — for example, a TV unit, a room or a living area.
  • Note the approximate wall area, if you know it.
  • Keep a photo or a simple note of the space ready to discuss, if available.
  • Compare materials before you decide: uPVC vs plywood

A site visit may be arranged to understand the space, requirements and measurements before final design and quotation.
