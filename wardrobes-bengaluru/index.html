What to decide before your wardrobe design

Available wall and clearances

Room width, ceiling height, skirting, switches and door swing all affect the usable wardrobe footprint.

Daily-use priorities

List what needs easy access and what can sit higher. This helps balance hanging space, shelves, drawers and loft storage.

Shutter and finish direction

Choose sliding or openable shutters based on available movement space, then select a finish that works with the room's palette.
