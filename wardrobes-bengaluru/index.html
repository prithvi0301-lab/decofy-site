What to decide before your wardrobe design

Available wall and clearances

Room width, ceiling height, skirting, switches and door swing all affect the usable wardrobe footprint.

Daily-use priorities

List what needs easy access and what can sit higher. This helps balance hanging space, shelves, drawers and loft storage.

Shutter and finish direction

Choose sliding or openable shutters based on available movement space, then select a finish that works with the room's palette.

What to check when comparing wardrobe quotes

Two wardrobe quotes can describe very different work. Compare these four parts of each one before you compare totals.

Storage layout

Ask for the hanging space, shelves, drawers and loft units to be listed, not only the overall width. The same wall can be planned with very different storage.

Shutters, tracks and hinges

Decofy's wardrobe body, shutters, shelves and back panels are made from uPVC panels. Tracks, hinges and other fittings are separate items, so ask which sliding track or hinge is specified, and whether replacement parts are available.

Finish and edges

Ask to see the exact finish in person and how exposed edges are finished.

Installation and warranty

Ask what installation includes and what must be ready on site. Ask for the warranty in writing and check which parts it covers. Decofy's 20-year uPVC panel warranty against discolouration and degradation applies to the uPVC panels.

What should I check when comparing wardrobe quotes?

Check four things in each quote: the shelf, drawer and hanging layout, the sliding-track or hinge specification, the finish and edge details, and the installation scope. Ask for the warranty in writing and check which parts it covers.

Yes. Decofy's wardrobes use uPVC panels for the cabinet body, shutters, shelves and back panels. The panels are 100% waterproof. They are also termite-proof. These properties refer to the panels, not to hardware, hinges, sliding tracks or other fittings.
