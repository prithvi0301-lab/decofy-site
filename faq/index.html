Ask about your project

Materials and care

Are Decofy uPVC panels waterproof and termite-proof?

Yes. The uPVC panels are 100% waterproof and termite-proof, so they do not absorb water or provide food for termites.

How should uPVC cabinets be cleaned?

For routine cleaning, use a soft damp cloth. Avoid harsh abrasives and follow the care guidance provided for the installed finishes and hardware.

Does uPVC only come in a plastic-looking finish?

Available finish options can include colours, grains and profiles. Review current samples with Decofy for the choices available to your project.

Warranty and installation

What does the 20-year warranty cover?

The 20-year warranty applies to uPVC panels and specifically covers discolouration and degradation. Confirm the full project-specific terms before approval.

How long does installation take?

Decofy's average installation time is 15 days. The exact schedule depends on the approved design, project scope and site readiness.

Does installation involve on-site carpentry?

The process is based on measured design and off-site preparation, followed by fitting at the customer site. Any project-specific site work is identified during planning.

Consultation and estimates

Which parts of Bengaluru do you serve?

Decofy operates as a service-area business serving customer project sites across Bengaluru. Share your locality in the enquiry form.

Is approximate budget required?

No. Budget is optional and never prevents submission. Leads below ₹2 lakh can submit normally.

What should I prepare for a consultation?

Your locality, requirement, rough room measurements if available, expected start date and a few photos can make the first conversation more useful.

How is kitchen cost estimated?

Layout, cabinet volume, finish, hardware, accessories, services and site conditions shape the estimate. See the cost guide for a fuller checklist.
