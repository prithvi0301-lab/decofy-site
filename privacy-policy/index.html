Privacy Policy

How Decofy handles your information

What we collect through decofy.in and Decofy enquiry forms, how we use it, and who else handles it. Last updated: 3 November 2026.

1. Who we are

Decofy is a smart uPVC interiors business serving homes across Bengaluru, India. We design and install modular kitchens, wardrobes, wall panels and interiors. This policy explains what personal information we collect through our website (www.decofy.in) and through Decofy enquiry forms on Facebook and Instagram, how we use it, who else handles it, and how you can contact us.

Contact for privacy questions: admin@decofy.in or +91 95911 28007.

2. What we collect

When you send an enquiry on our website (home page or consultation page) you give us your name, your phone number, your area or locality in Bengaluru, the product you are interested in (modular kitchen, wardrobes, uPVC interiors or uPVC wall panels), your expected start timeline and, if you choose to write it, a message about your space.

When you send an enquiry through a Decofy form on Facebook or Instagram you give us the details you enter in that form: your name, phone number (which you may be asked to confirm with a one-time code), email address, and your answers to the project questions (type of project, what you are looking for, project status, and when you plan to start). Meta passes these details to us.

When you visit our website, we and the service providers listed below receive technical information such as the pages you view, the page that referred you, the campaign tags in the link you clicked (for example utm_source and utm_campaign), and device, browser and approximate location information. Our website saves the first campaign tags, landing page and referrer in your browser's local and session storage and sends them to us with your enquiry, so we can see which page or advertisement your enquiry came from.

When you call, WhatsApp or email us, we receive whatever you choose to share.

3. How we use it

  • To respond to your enquiry and contact you about it.
  • To discuss your requirement, arrange a site visit or consultation, and prepare design inputs and estimates.
  • To keep a record of enquiries and follow-ups.
  • To understand which pages and advertisements lead to enquiries, and to improve our website and advertising.

We contact you by phone call, WhatsApp or email, using the details you gave us, about your enquiry. We do not sell your personal information.

4. Who handles your information

We use these providers to run our website and handle enquiries. They process information as described in their own policies.

  • Vercel hosts our website and processes website enquiry submissions.
  • Google: Google Sheets and Apps Script store our enquiry records and send our team a notification; Google Analytics measures website use, including events such as a submitted enquiry; Google Fonts supplies the fonts shown on our pages.
  • Meta (Facebook, Instagram, WhatsApp): the Meta Pixel on our website receives page views and a "Lead" event when an enquiry is submitted. Our Meta Pixel has automatic advanced matching turned on. This means that when you fill in a form on our website, Meta's tool may read details such as your name and phone number, convert them into a coded (hashed) form and send them to Meta, to help match website visitors with Meta accounts for measuring and delivering our advertising. Meta also operates the Facebook and Instagram enquiry forms and WhatsApp.

We may also disclose information where the law requires it.

5. Cookies and similar technologies

Google Analytics and the Meta Pixel may set cookies or use similar technologies on your device. Our website also uses your browser's local and session storage as described in section 2. You can block or delete cookies and site storage in your browser settings, and manage ad preferences in your Facebook and Google account settings.

6. WhatsApp

After you send a website enquiry you may choose "Continue on WhatsApp". This opens WhatsApp with a short pre-filled message that names only the page you were on. It does not include the details you entered in the form. Once you are in WhatsApp, WhatsApp's own privacy policy applies.

7. How long we keep information

We keep enquiry details for as long as we need them to follow up on your enquiry, manage any resulting project, and maintain our business records.

8. Your choices

You can ask us to tell you what personal information we hold about you, to correct it, to delete it, or to stop contacting you. Email admin@decofy.in or call +91 95911 28007 and tell us what you would like us to do.

9. Security

Our website is served over HTTPS. We take reasonable steps to protect the information we hold, but no method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

10. Children

Our website and enquiry forms are meant for adults planning a home project. They are not directed to children.

11. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update this page and the date at the top.
