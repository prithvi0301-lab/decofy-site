uPVC vs plywood: choose for the way the space is used

Both materials can form functional cabinetry. The better choice depends on exposure to water and termites, maintenance expectations, finish priorities and the quality of the complete build.

Discuss your material choice

Quick answer: is uPVC or plywood better?

There is no single winner. Decofy uPVC panels are 100% waterproof and termite-proof, backed by a 20-year warranty covering discolouration and degradation. For plywood or any other material, specifications and performance vary by grade, finish and supplier — confirm the exact specification and warranty before deciding. uPVC vs plywood for kitchen cabinets

Kitchen cabinets sit closest to daily water exposure — sinks, spills, steam and regular cleaning. Decofy uPVC is 100% waterproof and termite-proof; for any material, hardware, edge finishing and installation quality are also worth comparing. uPVC vs plywood for wardrobes

Wardrobes are used differently from kitchen cabinets — enclosed storage rather than direct water contact, with termite resistance as a key consideration. Decofy uPVC wardrobes are 100% waterproof and termite-proof, backed by a 20-year warranty covering discolouration and degradation. Where uPVC is especially useful

What the comparison does not decide

Material alone does not determine the quality of a kitchen or wardrobe. Layout, hardware, build accuracy, edge details, installation and after-sales terms still matter — see common material-selection mistakes to avoid.

Questions to ask any supplier

  • Which parts of the cabinet use the stated material?
  • What hardware and internal accessories are included?
  • What does the warranty cover and exclude?
  • How will site measurements and service points be verified?
  • What work must be complete before installation begins?
