uPVC vs plywood: choose for the way the space is used

Both materials can form functional cabinetry. The better choice depends on exposure to water and termites, maintenance expectations, finish priorities and the quality of the complete build.

Where uPVC is especially useful

Kitchen sink zones, utility areas and homes concerned about termites benefit from a material that does not absorb water and is not a food source for pests.

What the comparison does not decide

Material alone does not determine the quality of a kitchen or wardrobe. Layout, hardware, manufacturing accuracy, edge details, installation and after-sales terms still matter.

Questions to ask any supplier

  • Which parts of the cabinet use the stated material?
  • What hardware and internal accessories are included?
  • What does the warranty cover and exclude?
  • How will site measurements and service points be verified?
  • What work must be complete before installation begins?
