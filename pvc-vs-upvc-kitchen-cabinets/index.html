PVC vs uPVC kitchen cabinets: what is the difference?

uPVC is a rigid form of PVC, not an unrelated material. PVC is the broader material family; uPVC means unplasticised polyvinyl chloride. For kitchen cabinets, compare the actual panel and complete cabinet specification. The words “PVC” and “uPVC” alone cannot tell you which kitchen will perform better.

What do PVC and uPVC mean?

PVC stands for polyvinyl chloride. It can be formulated as a rigid or flexible material. Plasticisers are additives used to make PVC flexible; uPVC refers to its unplasticised, rigid form. See the European Council of Vinyl Manufacturers’ explanation of plasticisers.

Do not assume that a cabinet described simply as “PVC” must be soft or flexible. Ask the supplier to identify the exact panel product. Panel construction is a separate question from formulation: ask whether the quoted panel is solid, foamed or hollow, and which parts of the cabinet use it.

Which should you choose for your kitchen?

Choose a documented cabinet system that suits your layout, storage and cooking routine. If one quote says “PVC” and another says “uPVC”, first establish what each supplier is actually supplying. Then compare the cabinet construction and included fittings on the same basis.

For sink cabinets and wet areas

Ask how the sink cut-out, pipe openings and adjoining surfaces will be finished, and how a leak can be inspected and repaired. Waterproof panels do not fix leaking plumbing or protect every other component. Read our waterproof kitchen cabinet materials guide for the broader decision.

For shelves, drawers and daily use

Tell the supplier where you plan to store heavy cookware and appliances. Request the shelf-support arrangement, drawer load ratings and fixing method for the selected hardware. Do not infer a complete cabinet’s strength from the material name.

For the cooking zone

Have the supplier check the chosen hob, oven and chimney installation instructions before finalising nearby cabinets. Confirm ventilation, clearances and any specified protective details in the design. Avoid unsupported promises that an entire material category is heatproof.

For a fair price comparison

Match dimensions, carcass and shutter specifications, finishes, hinges, channels, accessories, worktop scope, installation and exclusions. A lower headline rate may cover a different scope. Use the Bengaluru modular kitchen cost guide to prepare your enquiry.

Before ordering: ask for these details in writing

  • Exact material: manufacturer, product name, panel construction and thickness for the carcass, shutters, shelves and back panels.
  • Complete scope: what is included in the kitchen, and which components use a different material.
  • Hardware: hinge and drawer specifications, load ratings and how the fittings are fixed to the panels.
  • Site details: sink plumbing access, wet-area finishing, appliance clearances and ventilation.
  • Finish sample: the actual finish proposed for your kitchen, with care instructions.
  • Itemised quotation: cabinet dimensions, accessories, installation, applicable taxes and exclusions.
  • Warranty: what is covered, what is excluded and how service requests are handled.

Quick answers about PVC and uPVC kitchen cabinets

Are PVC and uPVC the same material?

uPVC is unplasticised polyvinyl chloride, a rigid form of PVC. PVC is the broader material family and includes rigid and flexible formulations. Ask for the exact panel specification rather than judging a kitchen by the label alone.

Is every PVC kitchen cabinet made from flexible plastic?

No. PVC is not a synonym for flexible plastic. A cabinet described as PVC may use rigid panels. Confirm the panel formulation, construction and intended cabinet use with the supplier.

Which is better for kitchen cabinets: PVC or uPVC?

The names alone do not establish a winner. Compare the actual panels, cabinet construction, shelf supports, hinge fixings, hardware, installation details and written warranty. Decofy offers uPVC modular kitchens in Bengaluru.

Does waterproof uPVC make the entire kitchen waterproof?

No. Decofy uPVC panels are waterproof, but plumbing, joints, walls, hardware and other components still need appropriate installation and care. A waterproof panel does not prevent a plumbing leak.

Can uPVC cabinets be fitted near a hob or oven?

The layout must follow the appliance manufacturer’s clearance and ventilation instructions and the panel supplier’s installation guidance. Do not assume a PVC or uPVC label means heatproof or fireproof.

Is uPVC always more expensive than PVC kitchen cabinets?

There is no reliable price comparison from the names alone. Compare quotes for the same cabinet dimensions, panel specification, finishes, hardware, accessories, installation and exclusions.

What does Decofy’s 20-year warranty cover?

Decofy’s 20-year uPVC panel warranty covers discolouration and degradation. It is a panel warranty, not a blanket 20-year guarantee for hardware, appliances or every part of the kitchen. Ask for the written terms for your project.
