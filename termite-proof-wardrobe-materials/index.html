Termite-proof wardrobe materials: what to compare

For wardrobes, termite risk is the main long-term concern — timber-based boards may need preventive treatment, while uPVC does not provide a food source for termites by design. Decofy uPVC wardrobe panels are termite-proof; other materials can also work with the right treatment, sealing and hardware.

Discuss your wardrobe materials

What "termite-proof" means for a wardrobe

Termite-proof means the material itself is not a food source for termites, rather than being treated to discourage them. uPVC falls into this category — Decofy uPVC wardrobe panels are termite-proof and 100% waterproof by design. Timber-based boards like plywood are a food source unless the wood is treated and maintained, so their termite resistance depends on the species, treatment and how well the finished piece is sealed.

What else affects wardrobe durability, beyond material

Termite resistance is one factor among several. Storage layout affects how weight and moisture are distributed across shelves and hanging space. Finish choice affects both appearance and how well surfaces resist daily wear. Hardware quality determines how smoothly shutters, drawers and hinges operate over years of use. And room conditions — humidity, ventilation and direct sunlight — affect any material over time. Compare the full factor-by-factor breakdown in the uPVC vs plywood comparison.

Checklist for choosing termite-proof wardrobe materials

  • Confirm whether the material is termite-proof by composition, or termite-resistant through treatment.
  • Ask what treatment or preventive measures apply if a timber-based board is used.
  • Check hardware quality for shutters, hinges and drawer channels.
  • Review the room's humidity and ventilation conditions before finalising material and finish.
  • Confirm what the warranty covers and for how long.
