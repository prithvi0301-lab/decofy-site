What "termite-proof" means for a wardrobe

"Termite-proof" can mean different things depending on the material and supplier. Decofy uPVC wardrobe panels are termite-proof, backed by a 20-year warranty covering discolouration and degradation. For any other material, ask the supplier directly what makes it termite-resistant and get that confirmed in writing — rather than relying on a general material label.

What else affects wardrobe durability, beyond material

Termite resistance is one factor worth comparing. Ask about storage layout, finish and hardware quality, and how these were considered in the design. Compare these factors, not just the base material, in the full uPVC vs plywood comparison.

Checklist for choosing termite-proof wardrobe materials

  • Ask what makes the material termite-resistant, and get it confirmed in writing.
  • Ask for the exact specification and warranty for any non-uPVC material.
  • Check hardware quality for shutters, hinges and drawer channels.
  • Confirm what the warranty covers and for how long.
