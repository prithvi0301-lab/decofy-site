Mistakes to avoid when choosing kitchen and wardrobe materials

Most material-selection regrets come from comparing the wrong things — mismatched quote scopes, ignored water or termite risk, or a decision made on finish alone. Here are the common mistakes to check for before you commit.

Discuss your project

8 mistakes homeowners make

1. Choosing a material without checking water exposure

Sink zones, utility areas and humid rooms put different demands on a material than a dry bedroom wall. Match the material to where it will actually sit.

2. Ignoring termite risk for enclosed or humid spaces

Wardrobes and enclosed cabinetry can be as exposed to termite risk as they are to moisture. Confirm termite resistance alongside water resistance, not instead of it.

3. Comparing quotes with different scopes

One quote may include hardware, edge finishing and services that another excludes. Compare the same scope before comparing the price.

4. Choosing based on finish alone

A finish can be replicated across several base materials. Confirm what's underneath the finish before deciding.

5. Ignoring hardware and accessories

Hinges, channels and drawer mechanisms affect daily use as much as the panel material does.

6. Not checking exactly what the warranty covers

Confirm the scope, duration and exclusions of any warranty before approving a project, rather than assuming full coverage.

7. Assuming site readiness won't affect the schedule

Access, working hours, unfinished services and pending civil work can all affect installation sequencing and the confirmed schedule.

8. Treating "waterproof" or "termite-proof" as marketing words

Confirm what the material actually is — and what independent factors like hardware and installation add — rather than relying on the label alone.

Action checklist

  • List where the piece will sit and what it will be exposed to (water, humidity, termite risk).
  • Ask for the material specification, not just the material name.
  • Request quotes with an identical scope — hardware, accessories, edges and services.
  • Confirm warranty coverage and duration in writing.
  • Check site readiness and how it could affect the schedule.

Quick Answers

What's the most common mistake when choosing kitchen or wardrobe materials?

Comparing finish or price before checking water exposure, termite risk and warranty scope — the factors that affect how the material performs over time.

Why do kitchen and wardrobe quotes look so different?

Quotes often cover different scopes — hardware, accessories, edge finishing and services. Compare the same scope before comparing price.

Does a waterproof or termite-proof label guarantee performance?

Confirm what the material actually is and what the warranty covers, rather than relying on the label alone.

What should I check before approving an installation schedule?

Site access, working hours, unfinished services and pending civil work can all affect sequencing and the confirmed schedule.
